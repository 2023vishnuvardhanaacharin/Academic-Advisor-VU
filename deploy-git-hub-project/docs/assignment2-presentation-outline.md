# Assignment 2 Presentation Outline

## Slide 1 — Title and objective
Explain that the project extends the AI Academic Advisor into a multimodal, secure and agentic academic support system.

## Slide 2 — Problem and context
Academic students need reliable answers about eligibility, regulations, course planning and student-specific decisions, but human oversight remains essential.

## Slide 3 — Original solution and RAG foundation
Show how the base advisor works with document retrieval and synthetic student profiles.

## Slide 4 — Multimodal extension
Discuss text, infographic and audio outputs.

## Slide 5 — Agentic workflow
Present the five-agent pipeline and explain why it is useful for traceability.

## Slide 6 — Privacy and security audit
Explain sensitive data categories, attack scenarios and safeguards.

## Slide 7 — Legal and ethical review
Review the use of generated text, visual summaries and audio content with attribution and misuse protections.

## Slide 8 — Demo scenarios
Show representative student questions and system responses.

## Slide 9 — Limitations and future work
Acknowledge that AI can assist with academic guidance but cannot replace human academic advisors or official institutional decisions.

## Slide 10 — Conclusion
Emphasize that the strongest solution is a responsible AI advisor, not just a technically complex system.
